# Leachate characterization data

## Total organic carbon

`toc_summary.csv` was transcribed from the thesis table. The manuscript reports
total, inorganic, and organic carbon for two independent SCB and SCF extracts.

## ICP-OES

`icp_oes_summary.csv` reproduces the values and relative standard deviations
reported in the thesis. They are retained as screening results because final
dilution factors, reporting units, detection limits, and the status of the five
instrument rows labelled as replicates require confirmation by the analytical
laboratory before journal submission.

The earlier screening acquisition must not be combined with the final
acquisition without an explicit analytical justification.
