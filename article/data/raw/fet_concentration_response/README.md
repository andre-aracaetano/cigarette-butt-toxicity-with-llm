# Fish embryo toxicity concentration-response data

`dataset_todos_fet.xlsx` is the consolidated source workbook for the lethal and
hatching concentration-response analyses. Its treatment sheets are named SCB
and SCF to match the article terminology.

The workbook includes concentration, mortality, hatching at 72 and 96 hours
post-fertilization, egg counts, dates, removal flags, and observations. The
publication notebook applies documented experimental-quality fields rather than
removing observations by spreadsheet row number.
